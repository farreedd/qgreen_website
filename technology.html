Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Forecast • Optimize

Used for

  • • Load and renewable forecasting
  • • Predictive maintenance signals
  • • Optimization recommendations

What you get

  • • Forecast curves and alerts
  • • Risk scoring and prioritization
  • • Clear next-best actions

Augmented Reality (AR) Dashboards

Monitor • Guide

AR dashboards bring data closer to the real world — like having an instruction layer over equipment showing status and what to check.

Used for

  • • Field monitoring and inspection
  • • Guided maintenance workflows
  • • Faster diagnostics and reporting

What you get

  • • Live overlays and alerts
  • • Shared operational context
  • • Higher speed and accuracy

Computer Vision (CV)

Inspect • Verify

Computer vision allows systems to “see” using images — like how a phone can recognize faces or objects, but applied to assets and environments.

Used for

  • • Asset condition assessment
  • • Compliance monitoring
  • • Change detection over time

What you get

  • • Evidence-ready summaries
  • • Remote monitoring outputs
  • • Faster inspection cycles

Digital Twins

Plan • Simulate

A digital twin is a virtual copy of a real system. It helps test “what-if” decisions safely before deploying changes in the real world.

Used for

  • • Scenario planning and dispatch
  • • Program evaluation and savings
  • • Infrastructure planning

What you get

  • • “What-if” results and comparisons
  • • Planning dashboards and reports
  • • Better investment decisions
